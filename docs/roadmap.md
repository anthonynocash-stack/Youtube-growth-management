# Roadmap

## Included MVP

- Single-operator Google OAuth connection.
- Read-only channel and 28-day analytics summary.
- Local editorial calendar and manual workflow states.
- Deterministic, explainable content experiments.
- AI-assisted, creator-specific scripts and complete video production packs.
- Policy and security documentation.

## Before a public multi-user launch

- Encrypted token storage, tenant isolation, account deletion, and retention controls.
- Google OAuth consent screen verification and production domain configuration.
- CSRF protection, rate limiting, audit logs, backups, migrations, and dependency scanning.
- Accessibility review, localization, and privacy/legal review.
- User-configurable approval workflow and channel ownership checks.

## Later opportunities

- Thumbnail/title experiment journal using creator-entered results.
- Audience question clustering from creator-provided exports with privacy controls.
- Revenue reporting where the account and API permissions allow it.
- Licensed asset integrations, richer original visual generation, and creator-controlled upload scheduling after provider and rights workflows are reviewed.

The roadmap intentionally excludes fake engagement, automated comment campaigns, scraped-content publishing, and guaranteed growth or revenue claims.
