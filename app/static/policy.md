# YouTube policy guardrails

CreatorFlow is designed to support compliant channel operations, but it cannot certify that content or a channel complies with YouTube's policies. Policy interpretation and enforcement can change. Check the official guidance linked below before publishing or monetizing.

## Product guardrails

1. **No artificial engagement.** The product does not create views, subscribers, likes, comments, watch time, or traffic. It does not provide sub-for-sub, engagement exchange, view buying, viewbotting, or misleading traffic features.
2. **No spam automation.** It does not mass-post comments, community posts, or repetitive outreach.
3. **Original value first.** Idea prompts are planning aids. Every published video should add meaningful creator insight, education, reporting, or entertainment. Avoid interchangeable, template-driven bulk output.
4. **Rights and disclosure.** Creators must own or license their assets, avoid impersonation, and make required synthetic/altered-content disclosures.
5. **Human approval.** A person reviews the final script, footage, title, description, thumbnail, sponsorship disclosures, audience settings, and upload. There is no unattended publish action.
6. **No monetization promise.** Analytics estimates and recommendations cannot guarantee eligibility, ad suitability, revenue, or continued monetization.
